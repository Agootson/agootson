About this website

Stay updated with my day-to-day life.

I am not into sharing my personal life on the internet but I decided to build this platform for my colleagues, friends and family who have been urging me to update them about my life, be it academics, social and anything. So, since you're here, I'll consider you one of them. There are many ways to navigate the site but I, personally, won't be updating or publishing many articles that much. Since I don't like sharing my life publicly, the only section that will be updated frequently is the Gallery. This platform is essentially my little corner of the internet. It brings together different parts of my life, including my academic journey, projects, interests, experiences, and the things I choose to share along the way while still in India. You can explore my projects, learn more about the skills I'm developing, read whatever I occasionally decide to write, or simply browse through the Gallery, where I'll be sharing my pictures and moments I've chosen to document. I don't intend for this website to be a constantly updated social-media-style platform. It's more of a personal archive and a place where people who know me can keep up with what I'm doing without me having to post everything everywhere. The site will continue to evolve as I learn, build, experience new things, and decide what is worth putting here. You can read more about me
